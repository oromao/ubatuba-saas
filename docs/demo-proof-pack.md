# Demo Proof Pack

## Suggested Demo Flow

1. Open login and sign in with demo tenant.
2. Show dashboard executive.
3. Jump to observatory and demonstrate market concentration.
4. Open CTM parcel list and map.
5. Open PGV valuations report.
6. Issue a certificate and validate it.
7. Open works permit and business permit minimum flows.
8. Open 156 and environmental modules.
9. Open public works and cemetery minimum flows.

## Proof Artifacts

- Build logs: API and web builds passed locally.
- Service tests: certificates, permits, alerts, monitoring, mobile, environment, 156, public works, cemetery, dashboard, observatory.
- Screenshots: dashboard, map, CTM, PGV, observatory, permits, 156, environmental, public works, cemetery.

## Commercial Argument

The product is not a GIS toy.
It has:
- municipal identity and tenant separation
- process and document traceability
- certificate validation
- permit workflows
- field/citizen/operational modules
- executive and observatory views

That is enough to present as a serious municipal platform in a scoped bid.
